# Part 2: College Event Planning and Review — Practical Event Plan
**Candidate Submission for Mandatory Assignment Part 2**  
**Event Name:** *Technovate 2025: Annual Inter-College Innovation & Tech Summit*  
**Institution:** Indian Institute of Technology / National Engineering College Campus  
**Target Duration of Walkthrough Video:** 5–8 Minutes

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Event Audience

### A. Event Identity and Scale
- **Event Name:** Technovate 2025 (12th Edition).
- **Format:** 2-Day Hybrid Fest (Keynotes, Hackathon, Robotics Arena, and Startup Pitch Competition).
- **Target Audience:** Engineering and computer science undergraduates, postgraduate researchers, early-stage alumni founders, and corporate recruiters.
- **Audience Scale:**
  - Registered Participants: 1,450 students across 38 regional colleges.
  - Footfall (Auditorium & Arena): ~2,200 unique campus attendees over 48 hours.
  - Venue Capacity: Main Auditorium (900 seats), Tech Lab Hubs (4 labs x 60 seats), Central Exhibition Courtyard.

### B. Core Objective & Success Metrics
- **Primary Goal:** Provide student developers and designers with direct venture-pitch experience and industry recruiting access while maintaining financial break-even.
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
  - Hackathon submissions: Target 60 teams; achieved 72 working prototypes.
  - Industry sponsorship: Target INR 4,50,000; achieved INR 5,20,000.
  - Student satisfaction index: Net Promoter Score (NPS) > +55.

## 2. Timing and Pre-Production Timeline

### A. Date Selection & Academic Calendar Alignment
- **Event Dates:** 17–18 October 2025 (Friday & Saturday).
- **Rationale:** Positioned three weeks after mid-semester examinations and four weeks prior to end-semester lab submissions. Avoided monsoon season and major national holidays.

### B. 8-Week Preparation Milestone Schedule
- **Weeks 1–2 (T-8 to T-6):** Committee formation, faculty mentor sign-off, theme finalization, and initial corporate outreach.
- **Weeks 3–4 (T-5 to T-4):** Venue reservation, IT infrastructure audit (Wi-Fi bandwidth testing), and sponsor MOU execution.
- **Weeks 5–6 (T-3 to T-2):** Online registration portal launch, judge recruitment, and vendor procurement (catering, badges, AV).
- **Week 7 (T-1):** Preliminary hackathon ideation submission filtering, volunteer dry-runs, and emergency medical/security drills.
- **Week 8 (Event Week):** Stage build-up, AV soundchecks, badge printing, and mentor welcome briefing.

### C. Budget & Resource Allocation (INR 7,80,000 Total Outlay)
- **Sponsorship & Ticket Revenue:** INR 5,20,000 (Title sponsor: CloudScale; Associate: FinEdge) + INR 2,60,000 (Delegate registration fees).
- **Expense Breakdown:**
  1. Prize Pool & Hackathon Grants: INR 2,50,000 (32.1%)
  2. Venue AV, High-Speed Leased Line & Lighting: INR 1,80,000 (23.1%)
  3. Participant Food & Refreshments (2 Days): INR 1,60,000 (20.5%)
  4. Guest Accommodation & Keynote Travel: INR 90,000 (11.5%)
  5. Promotional Collateral, Banners & Swag Kits: INR 70,000 (9.0%)
  6. Contingency Reserve: INR 30,000 (3.8%)

## 3. Approvals, Compliance and Documentation

### A. Formal Institutional Approvals
1. **Dean of Student Affairs:** Approval for after-hours laboratory access (24-hour hackathon perimeter) and overnight security clearance.
2. **Campus Chief Security Officer:** Perimeter access control, parking logistics, and fire safety equipment inspection.
3. **Finance & Accounts Section:** Segregated bank sub-account for vendor disbursements and GST-compliant invoicing for sponsors.
4. **Estate Office:** Audio permits and power backup (125 kVA generator for server rooms).

### B. Operational Documentation Pack
- Detailed Run of Show (minute-by-minute coordinator schedule).
- Volunteer duty roster with designated emergency points of contact.
- Code of Conduct agreement accepted by participants during digital registration.
- Sponsor deliverable tracking matrix (logo placement, stage mentions, booth allocation).

## 4. Event Day Execution & Crisis Management

### A. Team Structure & Delegation
- **Lead Event Coordinator (Candidate Role):** Overall timeline governance, faculty interface, and sponsor relations.
- **Technical & Judging Lead:** Evaluation rubrics, hackathon GitHub repository tracking, and audio/video staging.
- **Logistics & Hospitality Lead:** Registration desk, catering flow, and speaker concierge.
- **Safety & Volunteer Coordinator:** 35 student volunteers organized into security, ushering, and participant support squads.

### B. High-Pressure Scenario Handled (Real Challenge)
- **Challenge:** At 2:00 AM on Day 2, the primary campus subnet experienced a switch crash due to concurrent container deployments by 70 hackathon teams.
- **Resolution:** As Lead Coordinator, immediately implemented our pre-negotiated fallback: diverted core submission grading traffic to a secondary leased-line Wi-Fi network and issued offline USB test suites to judges. Submissions were extended by 30 minutes, preventing participant panic and ensuring 100% fair evaluation.

## 5. Recording, Media Curation and Post-Production

### A. Production Shot List
- 4K multi-cam keynote recording (wide stage view + presenter close-up).
- Roving gimbal shots capturing team collaboration and hackathon coding sprints.
- Sponsor booth interviews and 30-second student testimonial soundbites.
- Drone overview of the central exhibition pavilion during lunch networking.

### B. Privacy & Identity Consent
- Registration terms included an explicit photographic release clause for promotional highlights.
- Clear opt-out stickers provided for participants preferring not to appear in close-up promotional media.

### C. Post-Production Deliverables
- 90-second dynamic recap aftermovie for social channels and future sponsor decks.
- High-resolution photo gallery released on campus portal within 48 hours.
- Archival full-length recordings of keynotes uploaded to the department YouTube channel.

---

## 6. Post-Event Review & Candidate Learnings

### A. Successes & Quantitative Outcomes
- **Financial Surplus:** Final expenses totaled INR 7,42,000 against INR 7,80,000 revenue, yielding a surplus of INR 38,000 deposited into the Student Tech Fund.
- **Recruitment Conversion:** 14 students received summer internship offers directly from participating tech sponsors.

### B. Lessons Learned & Future Improvements
1. **Automated Registration Queue:** Manual physical badge printing on Day 1 morning created a 25-minute bottleneck; future editions should mandate pre-printed QR code badges.
2. **Serverless Sandbox Enclosures:** Future hackathons will provision dedicated cloud credits rather than relying on local campus server infrastructure.
